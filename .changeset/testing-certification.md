**The top of the test pyramid is now two explicitly separate jobs — a scripted gate and an agentic certification — with suite health machinery so test suites are born fast and stay fast.** `/gsd:testing-strategy` records both jobs without conflating them: the smoke gate stays deterministic CI (3–7 flows, <5 min, agent-authored and agent-healed via the vendor-converged planner → generator → healer loop), while certification — validating the app in real conditions — is probe-detected onto a CERT-0/1/1-limited/2 ladder and is never a CI gate. Capability is measured, not assumed: a 5-command live probe (goto → snapshot → fill → click round-trip verified by effect → screenshot) records per-operation verdicts, third-party certifiers first launch under an isolated HOME with an instrumentation audit (the doctrine carries live receipts: one popular certifier's first launch silently instrumented three installed agent CLIs), and the certifying agent is never the building session. Certification runs in the verify loop before UAT (`workflow.certification`, default `required`): drivable checkpoints are auto-resolved with real evidence (`kind: agentic_certification`, `source: agentic`), judgment/auth/CAPTCHA items always escalate to the human, and every path — CERT-0, no-user-facing-surface, declined, `off` — records an explicit outcome line that a ship-preflight sweep summarizes per milestone. The operational substrate ships as four named policies: seeded test accounts, transport-enforced email safety with a per-vendor honesty table, spend-capped real-LLM smoke asserting shape never content, and honest OAuth (verified provider test modes, or one-time human auth with a persisted session). Certification failures feed back mechanically — "which fast test was missing?" routes to the strategy's new coverage-debt path so the pyramid catches forever what certification caught once.

Suites are born fast by configuration (current-API per-stack defaults; container reuse stated honestly as local-only) and re-evaluated by trigger, not heroics: the executor records `{test_count, wall_clock, containers_started}` into SUMMARY frontmatter, the transition step compares against the strategy's suite-health baseline (T1 tier-budget breach fires immediately; T2 ms/test trend, T3 container churn, and T4 growth-backstop schedule at milestone close, with volume-vs-regression discriminated), and `/gsd:testing-strategy --tune-up` runs the four-pass strategy-governed flow — profile, config/cache, suite audit against the strategy (never merely "made faster"), append-only re-baseline with the fix-class recorded. Validated realistically end to end: a live agentic certification of a fixture app through the new verify-work section (real headless browser driver, real evidence, sandbox containment proven), the tune-up flow driven against a deliberately degraded suite (a measured 26.6× wall-clock win with every change strategy-justified), and a diff-proven CERT-0 walk showing byte-equivalent UAT behavior when no driver exists.
